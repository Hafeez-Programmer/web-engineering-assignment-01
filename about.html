Our story

Nearly a century of firelight

From trapper's hut to lodge

In 1928 the Hale family built a single log cabin beside Alder Creek. Over four generations it grew, beam by beam, into the lodge you see today.

We still use timber from our own managed forest and stone from the valley floor.
