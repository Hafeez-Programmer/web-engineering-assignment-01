Frequently asked questions

Good to know

What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in from 3pm, check-out by 11am. Late checkout is free when you book direct.

Is breakfast included?

Yes, every stay includes a full breakfast in the Breakfast Hall.

Are pets welcome?

Dogs are welcome in Forest Cabins for $40 per night.

How do I get there?

We are 2 hours from Cedar City airport. We offer shuttle transfers on request.

Is the lodge family friendly?

Absolutely. Children are welcome everywhere except the pools after 7pm.

What is your cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 7 days before arrival.
